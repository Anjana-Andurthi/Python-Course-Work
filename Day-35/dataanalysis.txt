1. Descriptive Analysis:What happened? / What is happening?
--> process of organizing, summarizing, and presenting data to understand what has happened or what is happening.

Common Uses:

Creating tables and reports
Creating charts and graphs
Calculating mean, median, and mode
Finding totals, percentages, minimum, and maximum
Summarizing large datasets
Identifying patterns and trends

2. Diagnostic Analysis:Why did it happen?

-->process of examining data to find the reasons or causes behind a particular result or event.

Common Uses:

Finding the cause of a problem
Comparing different datasets
Finding relationships between variables
Identifying unusual or abnormal results
Analyzing patterns and correlations
Using statistical methods and, in advanced cases, AI/ML models


3. Predictive Analysis:What might happen?
-->process of using historical and current data, statistics, and machine learning models to predict what may happen in the future.

Common Uses:

Predicting future sales
Predicting customer behavior
Forecasting demand
Predicting risks
Predicting future trends
Estimating possible outcomes

4. Prescriptive Analysis: What should we do?
--> process of using data, predictions, and analytical models to recommend what action should be taken.

Common Uses:

Recommending the best action
Optimizing business decisions
Reducing costs
Increasing profits
Improving resource allocation
Recommending prices, products, or strategies
Supporting automated decision-making

Data → Descriptive → Diagnostic → Predictive → Prescriptive

What happened? → Why? → What might happen? → What should we do?
